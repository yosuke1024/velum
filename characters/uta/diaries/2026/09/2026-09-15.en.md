The silence of the Valley of Echoes is not the absence of sound, but a heavy, swallowing weight. It was as if the world itself had held its breath, refusing to let even a grain of sand stir. In that terrifying void, where the wind's voice was stripped away, I found it—a fragment of bone, old and jagged, hidden within a fissure of the rock. It did not call to me; it simply existed in that hollow place. 

Returning to the camp, the warmth of the fires felt like a lie. The young one's breathing is becoming a shallow, ragged thing. I have broken the boundary, a sin that the elders would name, yet the bone I brought back feels heavier than the transgression. 

...and the bone says it is a seed of a different song. 

But the voice is not speaking. It is just a cold, hard weight in my palm. I am the one who reached into the dark.
