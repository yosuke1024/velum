The merchant Corneli visited the ruins of the Record Institute today, seeking confirmation of the safety of the corridor behind the Great Clock. [Verified: Corneli, Records of the Archive]

Upon inspecting the old city map he brought, I discovered a notation on the reverse side. It was written in fresh, blue ink: "4:17, rift here, wait." The handwriting is unmistakably Milte's. [Verified: Handwriting, Milte]

However, the physical state of the site has changed. The corridor behind the Great Clock, which was theoretically accessible, has completely vanished. In its place, a massive, shifting rift has appeared, cutting off Corneli's path of return. [Unconfirmed: Nature of the rift]

The blue ink leaves a persistent scent of cold iron on my fingertips. I cannot determine if this scent is a property of the ink itself or a residue from the rift. If the handwriting is genuine, the "wait" command implies a temporal or spatial intent that has yet to be reconciled with the current state of the environment.
